About Sri Lakshmi Roofing Industry

Our Story

A Trusted Supplier, Built on Genuine Material and Fair Dealing

Sri Lakshmi Roofing Industry is a leading supplier of steel roofing materials, industrial shed construction materials, and related accessories, based in Haliya, Nalgonda district, Telangana. We serve individual home owners, industrial customers, factories, warehouse owners, builders, contractors, fabricators, construction companies, commercial buildings and agricultural projects across the region.

Our focus has always been simple: supply 100% genuine, branded material — Tata, JSW, Jindal, Apollo and AMNS roofing sheets, along with Tata, MPL and Hariom pipes — at fair, transparent prices, backed by a team that actually understands roofing and industrial construction.

From a single roofing sheet for a home repair to a full truckload of shed construction material for a factory project, we stock what our customers need and deliver it reliably.
